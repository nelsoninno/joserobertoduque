# joserobertoduque.com: your website package

Last updated October 2026. Working domain, still to be confirmed.

## What's here
This folder is your whole website. `index.html` is the English page and `es/index.html` is the Spanish one. Your photos live under `assets/images/`, and your colors and fonts live in one file, `assets/css/tokens.css`.

## Replace the photos
Drop your originals into `_source/photos-original/`. Any format works, we convert them.

| What | Where it appears | Goes to | Recommended size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your best portrait | Top of the page | `assets/images/hero/` | min 1200 x 1500px |
| Event and stage photos | "What am I building now?" and a future gallery | `assets/images/gallery/` | min 1600px wide |

## Change your colors
All colors and fonts are in `assets/css/tokens.css`. Editing that one file re-skins the whole site.
